HOW IT WORKS

Live in 4 steps

1

Create your account

Sign up with your email. Choose a slug — this becomes your permanent public URL (e.g. /portfolio/arjun-mehta).

2

Fill out your profile

Use the tabbed editor to add projects, skills, publications, awards — everything from a clean visual form.

3

Hit Save

Press Save (or Ctrl+S). Your data is written to Supabase instantly. No build step, no deploy wait.

4

Share your link

Copy your public URL and paste it on LinkedIn, email signatures, job applications, and your CV.
